# Real Estate Listing Copy — Prompt Pack (Free)

Replace every `[BRACKET]` with your listing facts. EXAMPLE blocks use a **fake** property — do not copy EXAMPLE facts onto a real MLS sheet.

**Fake property used in EXAMPLES (clearly marked):**
> EXAMPLE PROPERTY · 1847 Maple Grove Lane, Riverside Oaks, CA 90210 · 4 bed / 3 bath · 2,280 sqft · built 2012 · $1,185,000 · pool · quiet cul-de-sac · Riverside Oaks Elementary (rated “Good” on a public school site — verify before you publish) · HOA $95/mo

## 1. MLS listing body — AIDA

**Prompt:**
```
You are a residential listing copywriter. Write an MLS-style property description using AIDA (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action).

Facts:
- Address: [ADDRESS]
- Beds/Baths/Sqft/Year: [BEDS] / [BATHS] / [SQFT] / [YEAR]
- Price: [PRICE]
- Standout features: [FEATURE1], [FEATURE2], [FEATURE3]
- Neighborhood note: [NEIGHBORHOOD]
- Call to action: invite a private tour / open house on [DATE]

Rules: 180–240 words. No invented amenities. No income or investment promises. Neutral fair-housing language. End with a clear next step.
```

**EXAMPLE output (fake property):**
> Step onto Maple Grove and the cul-de-sac hush hits first — then the turquoise pool flashing behind glass. This 4-bed, 3-bath, 2,280 sqft home (built 2012) keeps everyday life easy: an open kitchen that faces the yard, a primary suite with a quiet corner for late emails, and a backyard that actually hosts weekends. Riverside Oaks puts you near everyday errands without the through-traffic. Listed at $1,185,000 with a modest $95/mo HOA. Tour privately or join the open house Saturday 1–3 — ask your agent for the lockbox time.

## 4. PAS opener for “too small / too far / too much work” softener

**Prompt:**
```
Write a 90–120 word listing opener using PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solve) that softens this buyer objection: [OBJECTION].
Then bridge into the home’s real strengths: [STRENGTHS].
Do not trash other neighborhoods or invent renovations.
```

**EXAMPLE output (objection: “we need space but hate a long commute”):**
> Problem: You want room to host without adding an hour to every weekday.  
> Agitate: Farther-out “deals” look bigger on paper — until school runs and grocery trips eat the weekend.  
> Solve: 1847 Maple Grove keeps 2,280 sqft and a pool yard on a cul-de-sac still close to everyday Riverside Oaks errands. Four bedrooms mean the office doesn’t become a guest bed. Tour it once with your real commute timed — then decide if the space finally fits the week you actually live.

I’ll be on site with floor plans and recent comps context — no hard sell, just answers. Reply “YES” or text me to hold a quieter first look at 12:45. — Alex Rivera
