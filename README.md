# Real Estate Listing Copy Prompt Pack (Free Sample)

**What:** 16 ready-to-paste AI prompts that write MLS listing descriptions, open-house blurbs, and social captions for residential real estate agents.

**Who for:** Solo or small-team residential agents who need listing copy fast — not generic “500 ChatGPT prompts” dumps.

**Price:** **$0** (this free pack). Download / clone / copy. No signup.

**Why trust:** Prompts are built on widely taught public marketing methods — **AIDA**, **PAS** (Problem–Agitate–Solve), **feature → benefit**, and **open-house urgency** — not on one person’s private playbook. Each prompt includes a short **EXAMPLE** output against a clearly marked fake property so you can judge quality before you paste.

**After click:** Open `prompts.md`, copy one prompt, fill the `[brackets]`, paste into your AI tool of choice. Instant draft text you edit before publishing.

## How to use (60 seconds)

1. Open `prompts.md`.
2. Pick the job you need (e.g. “MLS listing — AIDA”).
3. Replace every `[BRACKET]` with your real address, beds, baths, sqft, price, school, HOA, etc.
4. Paste into ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini / your preferred model.
5. Edit facts, fair-housing language, and brokerage rules before you publish.

**Fair housing / compliance:** These are drafting aids. You (or your brokerage) are responsible for final copy, disclosures, and local advertising rules. Do not invent amenities, views, or school ratings.
